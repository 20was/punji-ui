Canvas chart adapters (experimental): two optional subpaths that put a third-party canvas chart inside ChartA11yShell. `@punji/ui/lightweight-charts` has `LightweightChart` (TradingView Lightweight Charts v5: named figure, keyboard cursor that drives the crosshair, live region and hidden data table from exact bigint prices; streaming `ref.tick()` and `ref.update()`), `lightweightChartOptions`, `lightweightSeriesOptions`, `moneyPriceFormat`, `mergeTickIntoCandle`, `readChartTokens`, `watchChartTheme` and `LIGHTWEIGHT_CHARTS_NOTICE`. The TradingView attribution logo is on by default, as the library's Apache-2.0 licence requires a notice and a link to tradingview.com. `@punji/ui/echarts` has `EChart`, `punjiEChartsTheme` (a theme generated from the Punji tokens, ECharts' own aria off), `moneyAxisFormatter` and `moneyTooltipFormatter`. Colours are read from the CSS variables and re-applied when light, dark or high contrast changes; money prints from bigint minor units. The adapters import nothing from the libraries; `lightweight-charts` and `echarts` are optional peer dependencies, and nothing in the root export or the core bundle loads them.

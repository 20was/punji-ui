Derivatives charts, a KLineChart adapter and two calendars. `PayoffChart` (`@punji/ui/payoff-chart`, beta) draws the profit or loss at expiry of a multi-leg options strategy (long or short calls, puts and stock, each with a quantity, a multiplier, a strike and a premium as `Money`): an exact line through every strike, breakevens as exact ratios (rounded once, starred when rounded), the maximum profit and loss with "Unlimited" as a word, a current-price marker, and an optional dashed "today" line from values the caller priced (no pricing model ships). Profit sits above the zero line, labelled and signed, loss below it, hatched, labelled and signed; it never relies on colour. The pure `payoffAt`, `legPayoffAt`, `strategyBreakevens`, `strategyExtremes`, `payoffSamples`, `payoffKnots` and `payoffSlopeAbove` are in `@punji/ui/chart-payoff` (bigint, no React). `VolatilitySmile` (`@punji/ui/volatility-smile`, beta) plots implied volatility by strike, delta or moneyness for one or more expiries, each with its own marker shape and dash, over a real expiry-by-strike table that prints every IV; you supply the IVs as percent decimal strings, there is no pricing model, and `smileGrid` returns the exact grid. `KLineChart` (`@punji/ui/klinecharts`, experimental) mounts a KLineChart v10 canvas inside `ChartA11yShell` with Punji tokens as styles (re-applied on theme change), exact money on the axis, crosshair and tooltip, streaming `ref.tick()` and `ref.update()`, and a named figure, keyboard cursor, live region and hidden table from bigint prices; `klinecharts` is an optional peer dependency the adapter never imports, and KLineChart is Apache-2.0 with no on-screen attribution (see THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). `EconomicCalendar` (`@punji/ui/economic-calendar`, beta) and `EarningsCalendar` (`@punji/ui/earnings-calendar`, beta) group releases and results by day in a required, printed time zone, with importance in words, actual against forecast and previous (EPS and revenue for earnings) compared exactly in bigint with "Beat", "Missed" or "In line" and the gap and its percent, "Awaiting" for what is not out, a session ("Before open", "After close") instead of a guessed hour, and days in AD, BS or both through the library's own calendar with the "projected" marker for an unpublished BS month; each day is a `ScrollTable`. Additive; nothing breaks.

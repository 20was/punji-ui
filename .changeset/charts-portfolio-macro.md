Add six beta portfolio and macro charts, each on `ChartA11yShell` and on its own subpath so nobody pays for what they do not import. `@punji/ui/fan-chart`: `FanChart`, a projection as nested percentile bands (5th to 95th and 25th to 75th by default, `levels` to change them) from a start value with a solid median, end labels that never collide, an optional goal placed among the percentiles, and exact decimals or `Money` in bigint. `@punji/ui/rolling-returns-chart`: `RollingReturnsChart`, the return over every trailing N periods (optionally annualised) or the rolling drawdown, as a line with a zero baseline, up to four series, and the latest, best, worst and share-above-zero printed. `@punji/ui/risk-return-scatter`: `RiskReturnScatter`, risk against return with named points whose labels are spread apart, quadrant guides in words, an optional benchmark and frontier line, and marker shapes for groups. `@punji/ui/yield-curve-chart`: `YieldCurveChart`, yield by maturity from 1M to 30Y with comparison curves, an inversion shaded and named in words, spreads in basis points, and a `term-structure` mode (contango and backwardation). `@punji/ui/style-grid`: `StyleGrid`, a generic 3 by 3 size by style grid with the selected cell, optional exact weights and a real table. `@punji/ui/seasonality-chart`: `SeasonalityChart`, average return by month across years with a min to max range and hit rate, on the AD or BS calendar using `MonthGrid`'s month names. `@punji/ui/chart-math` gains `rollingReturns`, `rollingDrawdown`, `seasonalityStats`, `parseMaturity`, `curveSlopes` and `labelClusters`, and now owns `drawdownOf` and `spreadLabels` (still exported from `@punji/ui/performance-chart`). The data comes from the app; the charts compute no forecasts.

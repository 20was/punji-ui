Beta price-only charts and breadth lines. Pure builders in `@punji/ui/chart-price-only` take prices as decimal strings, do their arithmetic in scaled bigint and return decimal strings: `renkoBricks` (fixed or ATR box, close or high and low), `kagiLines` (fixed or percent reversal, yang and yin split where a line passes the previous shoulder or waist), `pointAndFigure` (box size, reversal count, X and O columns), `lineBreak` (N-line, default 3) and `rangeBars` (fixed range). Every element lists the source bars and dates it covers, because time is dropped. One renderer, `PriceOnlyChart` (`@punji/ui/price-only-chart`), takes the bars and a type descriptor, each from its own subpath so a page ships only the type it draws: `renkoType` (`renko-type`), `kagiType` (`kagi-type`), `pointFigureType` (`point-figure-type`), `lineBreakType` (`line-break-type`) and `rangeBarsType` (`range-bars-type`). It is built on `ChartA11yShell`: a keyboard cursor across elements, a readout, a live region, a spoken summary and a hidden table with the source dates. Up and down are told apart by shape (hollow against solid bricks with triangles, X against O, thin against thick lines) and said in words, never by colour alone. `BreadthChart` (`@punji/ui/breadth-chart`) draws the advance-decline line or the share of stocks above their N-day average from the pure `advanceDeclineLine` and `percentAboveMA` helpers in `@punji/ui/chart-breadth`, comparing closes in bigint and printing the share as an exact decimal.

`CandlestickChart` gains beta trading-chart features, all off by default so existing charts draw and read as before, and moves onto `ChartA11yShell` (one focus stop, hint, spoken summary, live region, hidden table). `mode` picks how the price is drawn: `candles`, `hollow` (TradingView's rule), `bars`, `heikin-ashi`, `line`, `area`, `baseline` or `high-low`; `scale` is `linear`, `log`, `percent` or `indexed` (to 100) and `compare` draws other instruments over the price with a legend; `layers` takes `eventsLayer` (dividends, splits, earnings and your own marks on the time axis; Shift and the arrow keys jump between them), `levelsLayer` (support, resistance and cost-basis lines with labels that never overlap) and `baselineLayer`; `indicators` draws RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands and VWAP. New pure functions in `@punji/ui/chart-math` (`heikinAshi`, `priceTransform`, `logTicks`, `exactText`, `decimalsOf`; `spreadLabels` now lives here and stays importable from `performance-chart`) and `@punji/ui/chart-indicators` (`rsi`, `macd`, `bollinger`, `vwap`). Every opt-in feature is a descriptor from its own subpath and carries the shared engine, so a chart that imports none stays the plain chart: `heikinAshiTransform` (`heikin-ashi`), `priceScales` (`price-scales`), `eventsLayer` (`candlestick-events`), `levelsLayer` (`candlestick-levels`), `baselineLayer` (`candlestick-baseline`), `rsiIndicator` (`rsi-indicator`), `macdIndicator` (`macd-indicator`), `bollingerIndicator` (`bollinger-indicator`), `vwapIndicator` (`vwap-indicator`); `IndicatorPaneView` and `candlestickTrading` are exported for your own. Heikin Ashi and indicator figures are printed in exact decimals in the table and live region, and direction is never colour alone (words in the readout, live region and table; hollow bodies, marker shapes and line dashes in the drawing). The plain `candlestick-chart` module goes from 5.53 kB to 7.47 kB gzipped (the shared `ChartA11yShell` and the direction, hollow and line modes); the engine and each feature are paid only when imported.

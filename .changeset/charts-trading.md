`CandlestickChart` gains beta trading-chart features, all off by default so existing charts draw and read as before, and moves onto `ChartA11yShell` (one focus stop, hint, spoken summary, live region, hidden table). `mode` picks how the price is drawn: `candles`, `hollow` (TradingView's rule), `bars`, `heikin-ashi`, `line`, `area`, `baseline` or `high-low`; `scale` is `linear`, `log`, `percent` or `indexed` (to 100) and `compare` draws other instruments over the price with a legend; `events` puts dividends, splits, earnings and your own marks on the time axis (Shift and the arrow keys jump between them); `levels` draws support, resistance and cost-basis lines with labels that never overlap; `indicators` draws RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands and VWAP. New pure functions in `@punji/ui/chart-math` (`heikinAshi`, `priceTransform`, `logTicks`, `exactText`, `decimalsOf`; `spreadLabels` now lives here and stays importable from `performance-chart`) and `@punji/ui/chart-indicators` (`rsi`, `macd`, `bollinger`, `vwap`). Indicators are descriptors from their own subpaths (`rsiIndicator` from `@punji/ui/rsi-indicator`, `macdIndicator` from `macd-indicator`, `bollingerIndicator` from `bollinger-indicator`, `vwapIndicator` from `vwap-indicator`, and `IndicatorPaneView` from `indicator-pane-view`), so a chart that imports none ships none of their maths or pane drawing. Heikin Ashi and indicator figures are printed in exact decimals in the table and live region, and direction is never colour alone (words in the readout, live region and table; hollow bodies, marker shapes and line dashes in the drawing). The `candlestick-chart` module grows from 5.53 kB to 11.34 kB gzipped because the modes, scales, compare, events and levels live in the chart itself.

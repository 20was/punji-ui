Inputs, overlays and feedback, mirrored from the Punji web app: the Field
family (`Field`, `NumberField`, `DateField`, `TextAreaField`, `SelectField`,
`SymbolField`), `CompanySearch`, `ListFilter`, `RangeSlider`, `InlineConfirm`,
`Sheet`, `ToastProvider` and `useToast`, `Dock`, `Drawer`, `CommandPalette`
(with `moveIndex` and `moveChip`), `Tooltip`, `InfoTip`, `FloatingTooltip`,
`IconHint`, `EmptyState`, `Note`, `ToneIcon`, `Skeleton` and `SkeletonText`.
The dock and toasts sit at the bottom of the viewport on phones (Punji lifts
them over its own tab bar; the package has none).

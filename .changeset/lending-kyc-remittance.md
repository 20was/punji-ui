Lending, deposits, KYC onboarding and remittance, Nepal-first and exact. The pure `@punji/ui/lending` module: `calculateEmi`, `amortise` (part-prepayments that shorten the tenure or lower the instalment) and `simulateDeposit` (fixed and recurring deposits, credited monthly or at maturity, with configurable TDS), all in bigint minor units with an explicit `RoundingMode`. The pure `@punji/ui/kyc` module: `validatePan` (the same nine-digit rule as `checkPan` in `@punji/ui/tax`), `validateCitizenshipNumber`, `validateIdNumber` and `formatPan`, with Devanagari digits accepted. Components: `LoanCalculator`, `AmortisationTable`, `DepositCalculator`, `InterestRateTable` (changes in words, AD or BS dates), `NepalAddressPicker` (Province, District, Local level and Ward; the 753 local levels ship in the separate `@punji/ui/nepal-address-data` subpath and load on demand), `IdNumberInput` (PAN or citizenship number; `onValueChange({ value, check })`, like `BoidInput`), `DocumentUpload`, `OnboardingWizard`, `RemittanceTracker` and `InsurancePremiumCard`. Each exports its English copy as `DEFAULT_<NAME>_LABELS` (for example `DEFAULT_LOAN_CALCULATOR_LABELS`) for translation through `labels`. The calculators announce a new result once typing pauses, in one sentence. Estimates and display only: nothing prices, verifies, uploads or pays. Styles in `css/lending.css`; see docs/LENDING.md.

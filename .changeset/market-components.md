Stock market components, props only and with no fetching: `StockTable` (with `sortQuotes`, `nextQuoteSort`), `MarketMovers` (`rankMovers`), `MarketIndices`, `MarketStatus` (`getMarketSession`, an example `nepseSchedule`), `PriceLimitIndicator` (`circuitLimits`, `limitPosition`), `Watchlist` and `MarketHeatmap` (`squarify`, `heatStep`). Each has a subpath export and a stylesheet included in `styles.css`. See docs/MARKET.md.

Screener filters for the web migration (wave 3, beta): `RangeEditor` and `FilterPopover` from `@punji/ui/range-editor` (From and To, an exact-decimal range slider, quick ranges, a chart slot and the live result on one action), the grouped `FilterMenu` from `@punji/ui/filter-menu` (groups in columns, a note on why items are off), `PresetTile` and `PresetRow` from `@punji/ui/preset-tile`, a generic `FilterBar` from `@punji/ui/filter-bar`, and URL state with any router: `filtersToSearchParams` and `searchParamsToFilters` from `@punji/ui/filter-url` (typed schema of range, multi-select and yes/no filters; exact decimal strings; stable order; round-trip tested) and `useUrlFilters` from `@punji/ui/use-url-filters`.

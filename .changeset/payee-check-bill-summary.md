Payments trust and bills: `PayeeCheck` (`@punji/ui/payee-check`), the Confirmation of Payee answer before a transfer (match, close match with the registered name, no match, unavailable, checking, error) with a required risk acknowledgement, plus `comparePayeeNames`, `payeeNameWords` and `payeeCheckAllowsProceed`; and `BillSummary` (`@punji/ui/bill-summary`), a biller's bill before it is paid (customer, period, charges, rebates and fines, the exact total and a due date in AD or Bikram Sambat) with loading, error and nothing-due states, plus `billTotal`.

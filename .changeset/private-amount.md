`PrivateAmount`, `AmountsToggle`, `useAmountsHidden` and `setAmountsHidden` from `@punji/ui/private-amount`: one persisted, cross-tab store that hides private amounts on this device, with an eye button. `MoneyDisplay` and `Figure` gain `masked` and `maskedLabel`: the amount is not rendered at all, and a screen reader hears "Amount hidden".

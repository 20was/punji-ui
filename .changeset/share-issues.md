Share issues and corporate actions. New pure `@punji/ui/share-issues`: `issueStatus`, `sortIssues`, `checkKitta`, `parseKitta`, `nearestValidKitta` and `issueAmount` for IPO, FPO, right, mutual fund and debenture issues; `checkBoid`, `boidParts`, `formatBoid` and `spokenBoid` for 16-digit BOIDs with the DP ID and client ID split; `entitledUnits`, `adjustedPrice` (the price after a book close for bonus and right, exact until one final rounding), `costBasisSteps`, `costBasisAfter` and `wacc` (average cost through buys, bonus, right and sales) and `corporateTimeline`, all in bigint paisa with an explicit `RoundingMode`. New components, each with its own subpath: `IssueCard` and `IssueList` (`issue-card`; status in words, days left, BS and AD dates, kitta rules, kind filter), `ApplyIssueForm` (`apply-issue-form`; kitta with min, max and multiple, exact amount, bank account and CRN, declaration, then a PIN or one-time code), `BoidInput` (`boid-input`), `AllotmentChecker` (`allotment-checker`; many BOIDs in one check, bad lines named before sending), `DematAccountCard` (`demat-account-card`; renewal warnings), `CorporateActionTimeline` (`corporate-action-timeline`) and `EdisConfirmation` (`edis-confirmation`). Styles are in `css/share-issues.css` and `styles.css`. The check-pack unpacked budget rises from 1350 to 1505 KiB; the Everything budget rises from 93.26 to 103.5 kB gzip and styles.css from 27.71 to 28.9 kB gzip.

Layout and keyboard: `SplitPane` (`@punji/ui/split-pane`), two panes with a WAI-ARIA window-splitter separator that moves by pointer or keyboard and stacks in a narrow container, and `useHotkeys` (`@punji/ui/use-hotkeys`, also in `@punji/ui/hooks`) with `formatHotkey`, `matchHotkey` and `parseHotkey`, for application shortcuts that never fire while the person types in a field.

Trust and safety kit. The pure `@punji/ui/trust` module (`detectSpoofing`, `skeleton`, `areConfusable`): UTS #39-style checks for look-alike letters, mixed scripts, bidi controls and invisible characters in payee names, with look-alike detection against trusted names. Components: `TransferReview` and `ConfirmAmount` (amount in figures and words from one `money` config, a spoofing-checked recipient that blocks confirm until acknowledged, changed fields marked in words), `PayeeVerification` (Confirmation/Verification of Payee results: match, close match, no match, unavailable), `HoldToConfirm` (press-and-hold or cooling-off friction), `SessionTimeoutWarning` (WCAG 2.2.1 warning with throttled announcements), `ScamWarning` (scenario-specific scam interstitial with `SCAM_SCENARIOS`) and `SecureField` (`secureFieldAttributes`: the right autocomplete per kind, and the value never written to a DOM attribute). Every component takes `className` and `testId`. Styles in `css/trust.css`; see docs/TRUST.md.

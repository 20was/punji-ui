Wave 2 forms. New `PercentageInput` (`@punji/ui/percentage-input`): a rate field whose value is an exact decimal string with Devanagari digits, `min`/`max`, a refused (never rounded) extra fraction digit and the rate in basis points as a bigint. New `OtpInput` (`@punji/ui/otp-input`): one box per character with paste and SMS autofill (`autocomplete="one-time-code"`), numeric or alphanumeric, masked option, one group label plus a name per box, always left to right. New `StatementUpload` (`@punji/ui/statement-upload`) with the pure `validateStatementFile`, `formatFileSize` and `matchesAccept`: choose or drop CSV/PDF statements with type, size, empty, duplicate and count checks, no network. `NumberField` gains `step`, `min`, `max` and `steppers` (an exact decimal spin button for lots and price ticks) instead of a separate quantity component, backed by the new `stepDecimal` and `compareDecimals` in `nepal/decimal`. `DateRangePicker` gains a `presets` prop and a `DateRangePresetList`; the pure `@punji/ui/date-range-presets` (`dateRangePresets`, `presetRange`, `shiftDate`) and `@punji/ui/date-range-presets/nepal` (`nepalDateRangePresets`, `nepalFiscalYearToDate`, Shrawan to Asar) compute Today, This week, This month, Last 30 days and fiscal year to date without `Date`. The check-pack unpacked budget rises from 1240 to 1310 KiB. The `fields` and `date-picker` size budgets rise (fields about 0.9 kB gzip) and the Everything and styles.css budgets rise by about 3 kB and 0.7 kB gzip.
